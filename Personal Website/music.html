MY SOUNDTRACK

Music is a part of my everyday life.

Whether I'm relaxing, travelling, gaming or just doing my own thing, music is something I always enjoy having around. I listen to different styles and I'm always looking for new artists and songs.

WHAT I LISTEN TO

My Music Taste

Rap is one of the main genres I listen to, especially when it comes to underground music. I enjoy discovering artists who have their own sound rather than always listening to the same popular songs.

I like music that has a different atmosphere, interesting production and a sound that stands out. Sometimes I might have one song on repeat for ages, while other times I spend time looking for something completely new.

MORE THAN JUST SOUND

Music & My Everyday Life

Music can completely change the atmosphere of whatever I'm doing. It can make a boring journey more enjoyable, help me relax after a long day or give me something to listen to while gaming.

I also like how everyone has their own music taste. You can learn a lot about someone through the artists and songs they enjoy. For me, discovering new music is always worth it.
