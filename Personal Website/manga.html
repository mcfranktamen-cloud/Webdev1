MY ENTERTAINMENT CORNER

Anime, Manga & Great Stories.

One of the things I enjoy doing in my spare time is watching anime and reading manga. I like getting invested in different characters, storylines and fictional worlds. Some series really know how to keep you watching episode after episode.

01 / ADVENTURE

One Piece

One Piece is one of those anime that has a world you can really get lost in. Following Luffy and the Straw Hat Pirates on their journey across the seas makes it an exciting series to watch.

What makes it interesting is the combination of comedy, action, emotional moments and friendships. The characters all have their own dreams and personalities, which makes the story enjoyable to follow.

What stands out to me:

  • The different islands and adventures.
  • The personalities of the Straw Hat crew.
  • The action and unexpected story moments.
  • The importance of friendship and loyalty.
Visit the official One Piece website →

02 / ACTION & SUPERNATURAL

Jujutsu Kaisen

Jujutsu Kaisen is another series I enjoy because of its action, animation and interesting characters. The story follows Yuji Itadori as he gets involved in the world of curses and jujutsu sorcerers.

One thing that makes this anime stand out is how intense its fights can become. The different abilities and techniques make the battles interesting, and the animation brings everything together.

Things I enjoy about it:

  • The fight scenes and animation.
  • The unique cursed techniques.
  • The different characters and their abilities.
  • The darker storyline and atmosphere.
Visit the official Jujutsu Kaisen website →

03 / SPORTS & COMPETITION

Blue Lock

Blue Lock is different from the other two because it focuses on football and competition. It follows a group of young players who are placed in an intense training programme to develop the world's greatest striker.

I like how competitive the series is. Every player wants to prove themselves, and the characters have different personalities and approaches to the game.

It also shows the importance of confidence, determination and believing in your own ability. Even though it is a sports anime, it has plenty of exciting moments.

My favourite aspects:

  • The competitive atmosphere.
  • The football matches and strategies.
  • The different player personalities.
  • The focus on ambition and self-improvement.
