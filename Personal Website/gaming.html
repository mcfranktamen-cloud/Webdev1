Gaming has always been part of my life.

Gaming is one of my favourite ways to spend my spare time. I've always enjoyed exploring different games, competing against other players and getting lost in different virtual worlds.

From playing on my Xbox to growing up with Nintendo consoles, gaming has given me some great memories over the years.

01 / XBOX GAMING

My Xbox Experience

Xbox is one of the main platforms I enjoy playing on. I like having access to different types of games, whether they involve exploring massive worlds, fighting other players or experiencing a good story.

One of the things I enjoy about gaming is how every game offers something different. Some games require patience and strategy, while others are all about quick reactions and having fun with other people.

MY CURRENT FAVOURITES

Games I Enjoy Playing

Elden Ring

Elden Ring is one of my personal favourite games. I enjoy the huge world, the different enemies and the feeling of discovering something new every time I play.

The difficulty also makes it rewarding. Defeating a difficult boss after several attempts feels like a proper achievement.

02 / MY NINTENDO MEMORIES

Nintendo Shaped My Childhood

Even though I enjoy playing on Xbox, Nintendo has a special place in my gaming memories. Growing up with Nintendo consoles introduced me to games that I could enjoy for hours.

The Wii U, Nintendo 3DS and Nintendo Switch each offered something different. I enjoyed how Nintendo focused on making games that were fun, creative and easy to share with other people.

Some of my favourite memories came from playing games with friends and family. These experiences helped me appreciate competition, teamwork and just having fun.

Consoles I Grew Up With

Wii U

The Wii U was a memorable console because of its unique controller and the different games available. It made gaming feel interactive and enjoyable.

Nintendo 3DS

The 3DS was great because I could enjoy games wherever I went. Its handheld design made it easy to pick up and play whenever I had some free time.

Nintendo Switch

The Switch combines handheld and television gaming. I like how flexible it is and how it continues Nintendo's focus on fun gaming experiences.

How Gaming Has Influenced Me

Looking back, gaming has been more than just entertainment for me. Playing different games has allowed me to experience different challenges, learn from mistakes and enjoy spending time with other people.

Nintendo especially reminds me of growing up and having fun without worrying too much about anything else. Those memories are something I will always appreciate.

Things gaming has taught me:

  • Patience when dealing with difficult challenges.
  • Learning from mistakes and trying again.
  • Being competitive while still enjoying the experience.
  • Appreciating teamwork and playing with others.
  • Exploring new ideas and different worlds.
